Selected Work · Prodigy

Cutting math question production time from 5 minutes to 1

Redesigned Prodigy’s internal authoring tool so content teams could ship curriculum-aligned questions in under a minute — scaling output from ~12 to 50+ questions per week.

TLDR

Redesigned Prodigy’s internal math question authoring tool to support complex problem creation and streamline content workflows, addressing limitations that restricted question difficulty and slowed production. Introduced structured templates and a clearer creation flow, enabling content teams to generate curriculum-aligned questions in under a minute while reducing cognitive load. Grounded in workflow analysis and domain understanding, the system improved both usability and consistency of content. This led to a significant increase in output, scaling production from ~12 to 50+ questions per week.

Decisions that mattered

Prodigy’s internal question creation tool had grown organically as the product expanded. The workflow for creating math questions was fragmented and inefficient, making it difficult for the content team to produce curriculum-aligned questions at the scale required by the platform. The previously built tool catered to a North American audience which learns at a different pace than Indian students.

Students wanted difficult questions and I offered to redesign and revamp the tool so that content creation is simplified, faster and the team can create more difficult questions that students love to solve.

Content creators faced several issues:

  • The workflow was complex and unintuitive.
  • The question structure was inconsistent — for example, a “draw a line” option was available but there was no option to hide multiple numbers on a line.
  • Question structures varied too much, with limited editing and review tools.
  • This left them with slow content production cycles.

Process

I worked closely with the content and product teams to understand how questions were authored and reviewed. Then, I opened maths textbooks from grade 4 until grade 10 — solved questions myself over a week and tried to understand how a question is formed. How exactly is a triangle made to ask a triangle-based question?

Once I understood the basics of how a math question itself is written, I simplified the entire process such that there is a certain level of automation embedded into the process and the options allow customisation to such an extent that content creators can make dozens of questions out of a single topic. Isosceles triangle? Single click to make the triangle, hide any side, any angle, submit and done.

I redesigned the internal authoring platform and introduced a structured system for creating math questions.

Impact

The redesigned workflow significantly improved the team’s ability to create content at scale.

Results

  • Question production increased from 12 to 50+ questions per week
  • Improved consistency and quality across curriculum content
  • Faster turnaround for new math content used in the game

Over 3–6 months, 72% of students solved more difficult math questions than they had before.
