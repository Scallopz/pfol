Selected Work · Slice

Designing Trust & Activation in a Credit Product

Led a research-driven overhaul of Slice’s onboarding, content, and core UX — reducing confusion, improving trust, and addressing high sign-up drop-offs among millennial users.

TLDR

Led a research-driven overhaul of a credit product’s onboarding, content, and core UX reducing confusion, improving trust, and addressing high sign-up drop-offs among millennial users.

Challenge and Context

The Slice app was a product people wanted but the experience was broken.

  • High drop-offs during sign-up
  • Low user trust in a financial product
  • Confusing content and communication
  • Difficult to buy a product on EMI
  • Poor understanding of credit usage and benefits

The core issue:

Users didn’t clearly understand the product, and didn’t trust it enough to engage deeply.

Approach

1. Multi-source research

Pulled insights from:

  • Play Store reviews
  • Customer care data & call recordings
  • User interviews
  • Usability surveys
  • Marketing data

Segmented users into:

  • Power users
  • Non-tech users
  • Drop-offs (pre/post signup)

2. Key insights

  • Users didn’t trust the product at first glance
  • Sign-up flow was lengthy, non-linear and did not offer clarity on document collection
  • Core features (like vouchers) had steep learning curves
  • Repayments — the most critical feature — lacked clarity
  • Communication (emails/SMS) was noisy and irrelevant

3. Design direction

Focused on:

  • Clarity over features
  • Trust through communication
  • Reducing cognitive load
  • Linear, predictable flows

Execution

  • Rebuilt information architecture
  • Redesigned onboarding & application flow
  • Introduced clear CTAs and structured navigation
  • Overhauled all product content and communication
  • Designed customizable, block-based homepage
  • Simplified critical flows like repayments, vouchers, and credit usage

Work was shipped incrementally in sprints, not as a big-bang release.

Impact

  • Reduced onboarding friction and confusion
  • Improved user understanding of credit and product features
  • Increased trust in a sensitive financial product
  • Established a scalable UX foundation for future features

The app went from 50k users to a million (currently at 10M) in a span of 1 year post app overhaul.
