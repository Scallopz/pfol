Selected Work · VIDA

Turning a service into a product enterprises actually use

Redesigned VIDA’s verification offering into a configurable Solutions Builder Platform — 800 of 1,100 invited customers actively using it.

TLDR

Redesigned VIDA’s verification offering into a configurable Solutions Builder Platform that gave enterprise clients full control over UX, workflows, and analytics, addressing key issues where clients had to expose VIDA’s flows and lacked visibility into user drop-offs. The platform enabled businesses to customize verification logic within their own onboarding experience while tracking performance through analytics, aligned with a tiered revenue model. This shift transformed VIDA from a service into a scalable product, resulting in strong adoption with 800 of 1100 invited customers actively using the platform.

Context

VIDA provides identity verification infrastructure for businesses across sectors: from banks and telecom networks to marketplaces and the level of verification varies by use case.

For example, a bank may require full identity and document verification, while a food delivery platform may only need lighter checks for onboarding delivery partners.

Decisions that mattered

Enterprise clients needed more than just verification.

They faced two key issues:

  • Lack of control over UX — Users were redirected into VIDA’s flows during onboarding, breaking the host product experience.
  • Limited visibility into drop-offs — Clients couldn’t clearly understand where users failed verification or why.

This created friction in onboarding and limited their ability to optimize conversion. Through user research and client conversations, I identified the need for a configurable system, not just a service.

Along with the exec team, I proposed building a Solutions Builder Platform that would allow clients to customize verification flows, retain control over user experience and access analytics to understand drop-offs and performance.

Solution

I led the redesign and development of VIDA’s offering into a self-serve platform that allowed users to:

  • Configure verification workflows — A SIM card provider and a food delivery app, both can define what they wish to verify.
  • Seamless integration within client UX — Add your own UI and UX to the flow without the user having to leave their platform.
  • Analytics dashboards for tracking user behavior and drop-offs — Know what happens at what stage: did your user drop off at ID verification or face ID?
  • Tiered pricing aligned with customization and usage — Pay as you use, straightforward pricing.
